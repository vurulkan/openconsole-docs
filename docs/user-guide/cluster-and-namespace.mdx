# Choosing a cluster and namespace

**You want to:** look at another cluster or another namespace.

## Your cluster

The cluster you work on is in the header. Click it, or press `c`:

<Screenshot id="cluster-switcher" alt="The cluster switcher open in the header, listing prod-eu (default) and staging, with staging selected" />

- The list has the clusters your permissions cover. The one marked **default** is where you start until you pick
  another.
- Picking a cluster switches **only you**. OpenConsole remembers it, on every device you sign in from.
- In the open list, `1`–`9` pick by position, `↑` / `↓` move and `Enter` selects.
- Every action you take is recorded with the cluster it ran on.

If your cluster is removed, or you lose every permission on it, you are moved back to the default.

## Your namespaces

The left panel lists the namespaces of the current cluster **in which you hold at least one permission**. Others
are not shown at all, not even their names. Type in the filter above the list (or press `n`) to narrow it down.
OpenConsole remembers the namespace you picked.

The tabs at the top of the Dashboard (Pods, Deployments, …, Secrets) also follow your permissions: you see a tab
only if you may list that kind of object in this namespace.

:::info[Can't see a cluster, namespace or tab?]
Your permissions decide all three. Ask your admin for a role that grants `list` on what you need, naming the
cluster and namespace, for example *"`pods:list` and `pods:logs` in `shop` on `prod-eu`"*. Changes apply on your
next click; you don't have to sign in again.
:::
