# Detail drawers

**You want to:** see everything about one workload without opening five screens.

Click a pod, Deployment, StatefulSet or DaemonSet in the list (or press `Enter` on a selected row). The drawer opens
on the right; `Esc` closes it.

## Pod

<Screenshot id="pod-drawer" alt="The drawer of pod checkout: summary, the container with its exit code explained, restart history, resource usage and conditions; YAML, Previous logs, Shell and Logs buttons at the bottom" />

- **Summary**: status, ready containers, restarts, node, pod IP, QoS class and owner (a link to the workload).
- **Containers**: image, state, requests and limits, probes, usage, and the last exit code with an explanation.
- **Restart history**, **Resource usage** (1 h / 6 h / 24 h, more with Prometheus), **Conditions** and **Events**,
  warnings first.
- Buttons at the bottom, as your permissions allow: **YAML**, **Previous logs**, **Shell**, **Logs**.

## Deployment, StatefulSet, DaemonSet

<Screenshot id="deployment-drawer" alt="The drawer of Deployment storefront: rollout Available with desired / updated / ready / available, strategy, revision and image, CPU and memory charts against the limits, its two pods, restart history; YAML, Scale, Restart and Logs buttons" />

- **Rollout**: desired, updated, ready and available replicas, strategy, revision and image. It turns red on
  `ProgressDeadlineExceeded` and says how many pods are failing.
- **Resource usage** of all its pods together, against their limits.
- **Pods**: each with node, restarts and status; click one to open it.
- **Restart history** of its pods, including pods it has already replaced.
- A Deployment also lists its **revisions** with their image and restart time (read only; there is no rollback).
- Buttons, as your permissions allow: **YAML**, **Scale**, **Restart**, **Logs** (all pods together).

:::info[Can't see it or can't do it?]
- A button is missing: you lack its permission in this namespace: `pods:logs`, `pods:exec`, `deployments:restart`,
  `deployments:scale` / `statefulsets:scale`, `<kind>:edit` for YAML changes.
- No revisions: the cluster connection can't read ReplicaSets; ask your admin.
- The node is not a link: only admins can open nodes.
:::
